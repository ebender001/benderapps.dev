About

Focused apps for people who learn and work in medicine.

Bender Apps is an independent studio building practical mobile tools for surgeons, trainees, clinicians, and curious learners, with a focus on cardiothoracic surgery and surgical education.

Who's behind Bender Apps

Bender Apps was founded by Edward M. Bender, MD, FACS, a cardiothoracic surgeon who spent more than three decades in clinical practice and academic surgery. He is a graduate of the University of Michigan and Wayne State University School of Medicine (Alpha Omega Alpha), completed general surgery residency at the University of Colorado, and trained in thoracic surgery in Edinburgh and at the University of Chicago. He is certified by the American Board of Thoracic Surgery and the American Board of Surgery.

His career included academic appointments at the University of Chicago and UT Southwestern, private practice in Dallas, Illinois, and Cape Girardeau, Missouri, and, most recently, Samaritan Health System in Corvallis, Oregon. From 2016 to 2025 he also served on the faculty of Stanford University's Department of Cardiothoracic Surgery, retiring as Clinical Professor.

Alongside surgery, he has long built software for the specialty. He is an Associate Editor of Innovations, CTSNet, and the Multimedia Manual of Cardiothoracic Surgery, has lectured on apps for resident training at the STS and AATS meetings, and wrote about the subject in Surgeon as Programmer (Annals of Thoracic Surgery, 2019). His apps have supported published research, including the University of Minnesota Donor Lung Quality Index and the LIONS PREY nodule malignancy score.

The idea behind Bender Apps is simple: the people who practice and teach surgery are well placed to build its tools, so these apps come from the clinical side of the table rather than from outside it.

What we make

Our apps are small and purposeful. Each one does a single job well: rehearsing an oral board case, preparing for a heart team conference or M&M, reviewing thoracic surgery self-assessment content, staging a cancer, estimating surgical risk, or sketching a bypass graft diagram.

The portfolio spans board and conference preparation, medical student OR readiness, cancer staging references, risk calculators, and literature tools. Many have been refined across years of releases, and newer apps use AI to make practice more realistic and responsive.

How we build

Focused over feature-heavy

No sprawling dashboards. Every app is designed around one task so you can open it, get what you need, and get back to work.

Education first

The aim is to help you prepare, practice, and learn. Our tools support your thinking; they never replace it.

Respectful of your data

Our apps are not designed to take patient-identifying information, and each has its own published privacy policy.

Honest about AI

Where an app uses AI, we say so. AI output can contain errors and should always be checked against trusted references.

Important notes

Bender Apps software is educational only. It does not provide patient-specific medical advice, is not a medical device, and does not replace clinical judgment, formal training, or supervision. Please do not enter patient-identifying information into any of our apps.
