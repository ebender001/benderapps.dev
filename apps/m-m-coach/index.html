About M&M Coach

M&M Coach helps surgical trainees prepare for morbidity and mortality conference. You start by dictating or typing a clinical case summary in your own words, and the app asks focused follow-up questions to clarify the chronology, key clinical details, decision points, management, and outcome.

When the case is ready, you review a structured preparation package: a polished case narrative, important discussion topics, likely faculty questions, and PubMed abstracts that are searched automatically for your case. In-progress and completed cases are kept together in one place.

You choose a specialty to tailor the experience, including cardiothoracic surgery, vascular surgery, and other surgical disciplines.

Key features

  • Dictate or type a case summary in your own words
  • Focused follow-up questions on chronology, key details, decision points, management, and outcome
  • Polished case narrative
  • Important discussion topics
  • Likely faculty questions
  • Automatically searched PubMed abstracts relevant to your case
  • In-progress and completed cases in one place
  • Specialty selection, including cardiothoracic and vascular surgery

Who it is for

Surgical trainees preparing to present a case at morbidity and mortality conference.

Important notes

Do not include patient identifiers or protected health information. M&M Coach is an educational preparation tool. It does not provide medical advice and does not replace clinical judgment, institutional policy, or supervision.
