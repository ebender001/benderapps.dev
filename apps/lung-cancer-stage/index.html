Lung Cancer Stage

A practical TNM staging reference for lung cancer education, based on the AJCC/IASLC 9th Edition.

View on the App Store All apps

About Lung Cancer Stage

The prognosis of lung cancer depends on its stage. Lung Cancer Stage calculates the stage of a tumor using the TNM classification of the AJCC and IASLC, 9th Edition. T describes the primary tumor, N describes whether regional lymph nodes are involved, and M describes whether the cancer has spread to distant sites.

Choose the T, N, and M categories and the app shows the calculated stage, for example IA1 for T1a, N0, M0. Reference screens in the app list the definition of each category.

Key features

  • Lung cancer stage calculation from T, N, and M categories
  • AJCC/IASLC 9th Edition TNM classification
  • Calculated stage shown with the selected T, N, and M
  • Built-in T, N, and M definitions for reference

Who it is for

Clinicians, trainees, and students learning lung cancer staging.
