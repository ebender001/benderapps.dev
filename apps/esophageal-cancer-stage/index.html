About Esophageal Cancer Stage

Esophageal Cancer Stage gives you a quick tool for establishing the stage of esophageal carcinoma using the American Joint Committee on Cancer (AJCC) 8th Edition. Choose the staging model, histology, and tumor location, then set the T, N, and M categories, and the app shows the calculated stage with a short description.

Esophageal cancer staging depends on more than tumor depth, nodal involvement, and metastatic disease. Histology, histologic grade, and the anatomic location of the primary tumor all affect prognosis, and the AJCC staging system accounts for them. The app builds those factors into the calculation and includes in-app reference screens for the T, N, and M definitions, histology, tumor location, and grade.

Key features

  • AJCC 8th Edition esophageal cancer staging
  • Clinical, pathologic, and post-neoadjuvant staging models
  • Adenocarcinoma and other histologies, with tumor location and grade
  • Calculated stage shown with the T, N, and M categories
  • Built-in reference for T, N, and M definitions, histology, tumor location, and grade

Who it is for

Clinicians, trainees, and students learning esophageal cancer staging.

Important notes

Educational use only. The app makes no claims of complete accuracy or prognostic significance and is a starting point for discussion. Diagnostic and treatment decisions should be made with appropriate healthcare providers.
