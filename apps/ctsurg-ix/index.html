About CTSurg IX

CTSurg IX is a self-assessment and review tool for cardiothoracic surgeons, trainees, and residents preparing for board examinations or keeping up their knowledge. It includes the full SESATS IX set of multiple-choice questions.

Each question has detailed answer choices, an explanatory critique, and supporting references, and many include radiographs, CT scans, or procedural media. You can choose how strictly questions are evaluated, which makes it easy to self-test or retry questions for reinforcement.

An optional subscription adds AI Updates, which give current clinical context, assess whether the original answer and critique still hold up, and summarize what has changed in cardiothoracic practice since the questions were written.

Key features

  • Full SESATS IX multiple-choice question set
  • Detailed critiques and references
  • Radiographs, CT scans, and procedural media
  • Adjustable strictness for self-testing
  • AI Updates on whether answers remain current (optional subscription)

Topics covered

  • Lung and chest wall disorders
  • Mediastinum and diaphragm
  • Acquired adult cardiac disease
  • Congenital heart disease
  • Critical care

Who it is for

Cardiothoracic surgeons, trainees, and residents.

Important notes

SESATS IX was originally published in 2012 by the American Board of Thoracic Surgery. Some content may be outdated and should be read in the context of current guidelines and practice.
