About Oral Boards AI

Oral Boards AI simulates the surgical oral board exam. A clinical scenario is presented the way an examiner would present it, and you respond by speaking or typing, just as you would on exam day.

The examiner adapts to what you say, asking follow-up questions based on your clinical reasoning. Each response is evaluated turn by turn against the key management points, and a debrief after the case shows what you covered, what you missed, and which errors were major or minor.

Cases are available in cardiothoracic surgery and general surgery, and new cases and specialties are being added.

Key features

  • Case-based oral exam simulation
  • Speech or text responses
  • Adaptive follow-up questions from the examiner
  • Turn-by-turn evaluation of management points
  • Structured post-case debrief: concepts covered, concepts missed, major and minor errors
  • Cardiothoracic and general surgery content

What you will practice

  • Recognition of critical diagnoses
  • Operative decision-making
  • Intraoperative management
  • Postoperative complications
  • Communication and clinical reasoning

Who it is for

Cardiothoracic surgery trainees, general surgery residents, and surgeons preparing for oral board certification.
