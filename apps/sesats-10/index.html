About SESATS 10

SESATS (Self Education Self Assessment in Thoracic Surgery) is a tool for training and practicing thoracic surgeons to test their cognitive skills in the field. This app presents the SESATS X question set as multiple-choice questions.

Topics span disorders of the lung and chest wall, the mediastinum, acquired and congenital heart disease, and critical care. Some questions include images, videos, or CT scans, and every question has an explanatory critique with literature references you can read after answering.

SESATS 10 is the third in a series of apps built to review previous SESATS examinations, following the SESATS VIII and SESATS IX apps.

Key features

  • Multiple-choice thoracic surgery questions
  • Explanatory critiques after every question
  • Literature references
  • Images, videos, and CT scans on selected questions

Topics covered

  • Lung and chest wall disorders
  • Mediastinum
  • Acquired and congenital heart disease
  • Critical care

Who it is for

Training and practicing thoracic surgeons.

Important notes

This app reflects the SESATS X question set. A newer SESATS version is the one used for maintenance of certification, so some content may be outdated and may not reflect the current state of thoracic surgery.
