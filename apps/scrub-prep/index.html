About Scrub Prep

Scrub Prep helps medical students prepare for surgical cases with focused case overviews, interactive questions, quick review, and a growing library of OR references. Whether you are starting your surgery clerkship or exploring a surgical specialty, it helps you build familiarity with what you will see and the questions you may be asked.

Choose a specialty, then enter the procedure you will be scrubbing on or tap a common case. Scrub Prep generates a student-level overview covering why the operation is performed, relevant anatomy, key operative steps, complications, and things to watch for in the OR.

Key features

  • Case preparation: a student-level overview for the procedure you will scrub on
  • Quiz Me: an interactive question-and-answer session tailored to your case
  • Rapid Fire: short, focused questions for a few spare minutes before scrubbing
  • Learn the OR: surgeon-reviewed references on first day etiquette, instruments, abbreviations, energy devices, positioning, and incisions
  • Saved cases you can search, sort, filter by specialty, and annotate with your own notes
  • Sources cited for each reference, collected on one Sources & References page

Who it is for

Medical students on surgery clerkships, and those exploring a surgical specialty.

Important notes

Scrub Prep is an educational tool. It does not provide patient-specific medical advice and does not replace clinical judgment, formal training, or supervision. Case preparations, Quiz Me, and Rapid Fire are AI-generated and may contain errors, so verify information with trusted medical references and your supervising team. Do not enter patient-identifying information.
