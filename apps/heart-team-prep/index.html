About Heart Team Prep

Heart Team Prep turns a cardiac surgery case into a structured, conference-ready report. You describe the case in your own words by dictating or typing, in de-identified clinical terms, and answer only the follow-up questions still needed.

The app then shows how three heart team members would read the same case: a cardiac surgeon, a non-interventional cardiologist, and an interventional cardiologist. Each gets a recommendation, reasoning, and the points where they would disagree, graded with the ACC/AHA Class of Recommendation and Level of Evidence framework.

Positions are backed by an on-demand PubMed search for supporting and opposing literature, designed to surface the longer-term data that some decisions, such as TAVR versus SAVR durability, depend on.

Key features

  • Dictate or type a case with no patient identifiers needed
  • Follow-up questions limited to what is still missing
  • Nine-section report: diagnosis, indication, missing information, operative strategy, alternatives, controversies, technical considerations, postoperative concerns, evidence and guideline topics
  • Three heart team perspectives on one case
  • ACC/AHA Class of Recommendation and Level of Evidence grading
  • On-demand PubMed search for supporting and opposing evidence
  • Built around cardiac surgery terminology and workflow

Who it is for

Cardiac surgery trainees preparing to present and defend a case.

Important notes

Heart Team Prep is an educational preparation tool. It does not provide medical advice and does not replace clinical judgment, institutional policy, or supervision. Do not enter patient names, institution names, dates of service, or other identifying information. Your first complete case preparation is free.
